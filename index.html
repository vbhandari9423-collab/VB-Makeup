About Me

5+ Years of Experience

Hello, I'm Vaishnavi Bhandari

A passionate makeup artist dedicated to enhancing your natural beauty and making you feel confident on your most special days.

With over 5 years of experience in the beauty industry, I have had the privilege of working with hundreds of clients for their weddings, photo shoots, fashion shows, and special events. My journey in makeup artistry began with a deep love for colors, textures, and the transformative power of cosmetics.

I specialize in bridal makeup, editorial looks, and special occasion glam. My approach is personalized – I believe that makeup should enhance your unique features while making you feel like the best version of yourself.
